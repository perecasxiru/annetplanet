ABOUT ME


My name is Anna Bach Valls, I live in Barcelona and I spend most of my time working, drawing and hiking. I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at university. By day, I work as a product analyst in the tech industry. This allows me to see the excitement, but also the everyday monotony, of working in an office in such a fast paced and creative industry. By night, I draw inspiration from my daily life and bring together my two greatest pleasures, art and humor, to give life to my alter ego of Annet Planet.
