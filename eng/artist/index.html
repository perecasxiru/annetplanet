ABOUT ME

My name is Anna Bach Valls, I live in Barcelona and I spend most of my time working, drawing and hiking. I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at university. By day, I work as a product analyst in the tech industry. This allows me to see the excitement, but also the everyday monotony, of working in an office in such a fast paced and creative industry. By night, I draw inspiration from my daily life and bring together my two greatest pleasures, art and humor, to give life to my alter ego of Annet Planet.

Hypatia Mars missions

Simulated Mars missions

I took part in Hypatia I and Hypatia II, two simulated Mars missions run at the Mars Desert Research Station in the Utah desert. I was the crew's resident artist, and Executive Officer on Hypatia II.

Les Exploradores de Mart

Children's book · Estrella Polar, 2025

A picture book I wrote and illustrated, inspired by the women of the Hypatia II crew. Astronauts are sent to Mars to repair an antenna hit by a meteorite and get the robots back in touch with Earth. A story to encourage girls towards science (in Catalan, ages 6–12).

The Martian Astronauts Card Game

Cooperative card game · Lastuf Games

A cooperative card game for 1–4 players (ages 6+, 15-minute games) where you form the crew of Hypatia I and complete Mars missions by remembering and matching cards across astronomy, engineering, biology and communication. I illustrated it.
